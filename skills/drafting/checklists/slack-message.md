# Checklist: Slack message

The source is the user's dictation or notes, plus any thread they pasted. Their position comes only from their words. Other people's positions stay theirs.

## Shape

- First sentence is what the reader needs to know or do.
- Two or three short paragraphs for a normal message. One paragraph if it fits.
- Status updates: bold label, colon, then the content. Backtick identifiers (ticket IDs, service names, branch names). No nested bold.
- Asks go last and are explicit: who, what, by when, if they gave a when.
- No headers. No bullet lists unless the message is a list by nature (three or more parallel items).

## Register

- Matches the channel. A DM to a teammate is looser than a post in an incident channel.
- Disagreement is stated plainly.
- No apology for length or for asking.
- No sign-off.

## Attribution checks

- Does not resolve open questions from the pasted thread on their behalf.
- Does not restate someone else's point as theirs.
- Does not add context or reasoning they did not give, even if the thread would benefit from it.
- Does not commit them to actions they did not say they would take.

## Before returning

- First sentence makes the point.
- Each claim traces to their words or is attributed by name.
- No em dashes, no banned words or patterns.
- Reads like something they typed.
