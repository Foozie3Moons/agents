# Checklist: Discord post

The source is the user's dictation or notes, plus anything they pasted. Their position comes only from their words.

## Shape

- First sentence is the point. Readers skim a channel.
- One to three short paragraphs. Longer than that belongs in a thread or a blog post.
- No headers. Bullets only if the post is a list by nature.
- Code and identifiers in backticks. Multi-line code in a fenced block.
- Links inline, bare. No "check it out here".
- If the post replies to someone, start from their point, not with a greeting.

## Register

- Casual. Contractions, shorthand, the occasional "honestly" are fine.
- Matches the channel. A dev channel is looser than an announcements channel.
- No hype. State what the thing is and what it does.
- No sign-off, no emoji unless they used one in the source.

## Attribution checks

- Does not claim results, metrics, or adoption they did not state.
- Does not add a "what's next" or a roadmap they did not give.
- Does not restate someone else's point as theirs.
- Keeps their hedges ("so far", "pretty well") as written.

## Before returning

- First sentence makes the point.
- Each claim traces to their words.
- No em dashes, no banned words or tics.
- Reads like something they typed into a channel.
