# Banned words

Vocabulary to strip from every draft. Sentence-level and structural patterns are in `tics.md`.

Add to this list whenever a new one shows up.

- footgun
- load-bearing
- delve
- robust
- leverage (as a verb)
- seamless, seamlessly
- genuinely
- straightforward
- honestly (as a sentence opener)
- it's worth noting
- great question
- em dashes and their substitutes: `—`, `–`, ` -- `

## Exemptions

Quoted text from other people and anything inside code spans or blockquotes is exempt. Do not edit other people's words to satisfy this list.
