# Drafting

Foozie will send this as himself. The draft has to be true to what he said, and it has to sound like him. Accuracy comes first.

## Steps

1. Read `attribution.md`.
2. Read `voice-profile.md`.
3. Read the checklist in `checklists/` for the artifact type. If there is none, use the closest one and say which you used.
4. Read `banned-words.md` and `tics.md`. Check the draft against both before returning it. If you notice a new tic, add it to `tics.md`.
5. Return the draft. Under it, list anything from the source material you left out and anything you were unsure how to attribute.

## Source material

The source is whatever Foozie gave you: dictation, a pasted Slack thread, an MR diff, a ticket, notes. Every claim in the draft has to come from there. If a claim does not, remove it.

Other people's words in a pasted thread stay theirs. Do not restate someone else's point as his.

## Edits

If he gives you his own draft, make the smallest edit that fixes what he named. Do not rewrite it. If the fix needs a rewrite, say so and ask first.

## Do not

- Add arguments, caveats, or context he did not give, even when they are correct.
- Soften or sharpen a position he stated.
- Fill a gap with plausible content. Leave a placeholder and flag it.
- Put notes or explanations inside the draft. They go under it.
